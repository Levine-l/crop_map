Overall agreement improves after adding Sentinel-1 SAR

Agreement is evaluated against held out CROME crop map labels. Across all four metrics, adding Sentinel-1 increases the score by about 0.074.

Overall model comparison against CROME

Measures. Overall agreement is the share of all matching labels; balanced accuracy averages recall across crop classes; Macro F1 gives each class equal weight; Weighted F1 weights classes by sample size. All scores range from 0 to 1.

Main reading. The gain is consistent across total, class balanced and F1 based measures, rather than being driven by a single headline metric.

Secondary comparison against UKCEH crop labels

Measure. The frozen CROME trained predictions are compared with harmonised UKCEH labels. These are agreement scores against a second crop map product, not independent ground truth accuracy.

Main reading. S1+S2 remains higher by about 0.070 across all four measures, so the direction of improvement is not unique to the CROME comparison.

Largest gains occur in weaker optical only classes

Some crops have less distinctive optical only signatures or greater within season similarity. Pulses, potatoes, maize and oilseed rape therefore have more scope to benefit from SAR structure and timing information.

Class level F1 change: S1+S2 minus S2 only

Measure. Bar length is the absolute increase in class F1 after adding Sentinel-1; it is not the final F1 score. Crops are ordered from largest to smallest gain.

Main reading. Pulses gain 0.130 F1, while winter barley gains 0.024. Sentinel-1 adds most value where the optical only classifier was comparatively weaker.

Robustness check: Random Forest sensitivity

The same training and test split was evaluated under 36 reasonable Random Forest settings. This checks whether the SAR result depends on one particular model configuration.

RF sensitivity: gain range across 36 paired settings

Settings. The grid varies tree count (300, 500, 800), feature sampling (sqrt, log2), minimum leaf size (1, 3, 5) and class weighting. Each line shows the minimum to maximum S1+S2 gain; the dot is the mean gain.

Main reading. All 36 paired settings improve for both overall agreement and Macro F1. The result supports robustness, not selection of a new best model.

SAR gains by crop profile zone

Zones are defined only from UKCEH crop composition, not from model performance. The comparison then asks whether adding Sentinel-1 reduces disagreement between the model and reference in each agricultural context.

Zone level disagreement reduction

Measure. Disagreement is the proportion of held out CROME samples where the model and reference label differ. SAR reduction is S2 only disagreement minus S1+S2 disagreement, in rate points.

Main reading. Disagreement falls in all three zones, with the largest reduction in the mixed wheat, barley and beet zone (0.084).

Clustering sensitivity summary

Silhouette measures cluster separation and compactness, with higher values preferred. The sample column reports the smallest and largest zone level held out test counts. Although k=2 has the highest silhouette, k=3 is retained because it gives a more informative agricultural interpretation without the fragmentation of k=4.

Zone summary

Centroid profiles are shares of the eight selected crops, not shares of total grid cell area. Grid cells describe spatial support; test samples are zone totals used for disagreement estimates.

Complete crop composition of the three zones

Measure. Each bar sums to 100% and shows the cluster centroid share of selected crop area across all eight analysed crops.

Main reading. Zone 1 is the most evenly mixed; Zone 2 shifts towards both barley types and beet; Zone 3 is distinguished by winter wheat occupying more than half of selected crop area.
